~ Our story ~

Hi, I'm Gina.

Cookie Central Memphis started in my childhood home. Baking oatmeal cookies with my daddy and sister and making artwork for my mama hold some of my most precious memories.

Cookie Central Memphis was created as a resulting combination of the two — creativity, sweets, family, and a whole lot of heart. Every cookie, every jar of fudge, every cinnamon roll is made fresh, by hand, with care for the person on the other end.
